# Data Retention Policy - Adyatech Solutions LLP

**Last Updated:** August 13, 2026
**Purpose:** Define retention periods for different types of personal data

## Overview

This policy establishes how long Adyatech Solutions LLP retains different categories of personal data, in accordance with business needs and legal requirements under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

## Retention Principles

1. **Minimization:** Retain data only as long as necessary for the stated purpose
2. **Security:** Securely delete data when retention period expires
3. **Compliance:** Consider legal and regulatory requirements before deletion
4. **Transparency:** Users can request data deletion before retention expires

## Retention Schedule by Data Category

### 1. Enquiry & Lead Data

**Sources:** Contact form, quote requests, phone enquiries

| Data Type | Retention Period | Reason |
|-----------|------------------|--------|
| Contact form submissions | 3 years from last interaction | Sales cycle, follow-up opportunities, business reference |
| Quote requests | 3 years from submission date | Project reference, pricing history, legal protection |
| Unresponsive leads | 1 year from last contact | Marketing outreach window |
| Converted leads (clients) | 7 years from project completion | Tax records, legal liability, reference |

**Early Deletion:** Users may request deletion via /privacy-request at any time

### 2. Client Project Data

**Sources:** Contracts, project communications, deliverables

| Data Type | Retention Period | Reason |
|-----------|------------------|--------|
| Project communications | 7 years from project completion | Legal liability, warranty support |
| Contract documents | 7 years from contract end | Legal requirement, tax records |
| Deliverables & source code | Per contract terms (typically 1-5 years) | Intellectual property, support obligations |
| Invoices & payment records | 7 years from transaction date | Tax law requirement |

**Note:** May be retained longer if required by specific contract terms

### 3. Website Analytics Data

**Source:** Google Analytics

| Data Type | Retention Period | Reason |
|-----------|------------------|--------|
| User-level analytics | 14 months (configurable) | Google Analytics default, year-over-year comparison |
| Aggregated analytics | Indefinite (anonymized) | Business intelligence, trend analysis |

**Configuration:** GA4 data retention set to 14 months

### 4. Marketing Data

**Source:** Meta Pixel, email campaigns

| Data Type | Retention Period | Reason |
|-----------|------------------|--------|
| Meta Pixel events | Per Meta policy (typically up to 2 years) | Platform limitation |
| Email campaign data | 3 years from last campaign | Marketing effectiveness analysis |
| Newsletter subscriptions | Until unsubscribe | Ongoing communication |

### 5. Cookie Consent Data

**Source:** Browser localStorage

| Data Type | Retention Period | Reason |
|-----------|------------------|--------|
| Consent choices | 30 days | User preference, then re-prompt |
| Consent timestamps | 30 days | Compliance tracking |

**Note:** Not stored server-side; client-side only

### 6. Employee & Contractor Data

**Sources:** HR records, payroll, contracts

| Data Type | Retention Period | Reason |
|-----------|------------------|--------|
| Employment records | 7 years from termination | Legal compliance, references |
| Payroll records | 7 years from financial year end | Tax law requirement |
| Performance reviews | 3 years from departure | Reference, legal protection |

### 7. Vendor & Supplier Data

**Sources:** Procurement, invoices, contracts

| Data Type | Retention Period | Reason |
|-----------|------------------|--------|
| Vendor contracts | 7 years from contract end | Legal liability, tax records |
| Invoices & receipts | 7 years from transaction date | Tax law requirement |

## Deletion Procedures

### Automated Deletion

- **Cookie consent:** Automatic expiry after 30 days (client-side)
- **Analytics data:** Automatic deletion per Google Analytics retention settings
- **Newsletter unsubscribes:** Immediate removal from mailing lists

### Manual Deletion

For other data types, deletion follows this process:

1. **Identify eligible records** based on retention schedule
2. **Verify no legal holds** (active disputes, investigations)
3. **Secure deletion** of data from primary systems
4. **Backup cleanup** within 30 days
5. **Confirmation log** of deletion

### User-Initiated Deletion

Users may request early deletion via /privacy-request:

1. **Verification** of identity
2. **Assessment** of legal obligations (e.g., active contracts, tax requirements)
3. **Partial deletion** of data not legally required
4. **Notification** to user of what was/was not deleted
5. **Confirmation** email sent

## Data Preservation Exceptions

Data may be retained beyond standard periods when:

1. **Legal requirement** – Active litigation, government investigation, regulatory request
2. **Contractual obligation** – Specific client contract requires longer retention
3. **Business necessity** – Ongoing relationship, warranty/support obligations
4. **Public interest** – Evidence of criminal activity or fraud

## Retention Schedule Review

This policy is reviewed:

- **When laws change** – Update based on new DPDP rules or court decisions
- **When business practices change** – New data sources or services
